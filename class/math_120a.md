
## Definitions

**Frenet-Serret Frame**

The tangent, normal, and binormal vectors to a curve:
$$
T(s) = \frac{dr}{ds}
$$
$$
N(s) = \frac{T'(s)}{||T'(s)||}
$$
$$
B(s) = T(s) \times N(s)
$$

**Frenet-Serret formulas**

$$
\frac{dT}{ds} = \kappa N
$$

$$
\frac{dN}{ds} = -\kappa T +\tau B
$$
$$
\frac{dB}{ds} = -\tau N
$$


($\kappa$ = curvature of the curve, $\tau$ is torsion)


**Osculating Plane**

The osculating plane
